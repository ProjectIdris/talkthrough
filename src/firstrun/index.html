Ask your screen.

Hold the keys, say your question, let go. Talkthrough looks at what is on your screen, answers out loud, and draws on top of it.

Nothing is recorded until you hold the keys.

How should it hear you?

It works straight away with Windows speech. You can make it clearer now or later in Settings.

Say something for three seconds.

Where do answers come from?

Talkthrough uses a model you already have. It looked on this computer for these.

After saving, try a question on a harmless screen to check the full setup. Hold the keys and ask.
